# Product Principles

**Project:** Otlob Platform (اطلب ولا تتعنى)  
**Document Type:** Product Bible — Product Principles  
**Phase:** 1.9  
**Status:** Mandatory  

---

## 1. Purpose

Product decision principles for اطلب ولا تتعنى. When roadmap items conflict, these principles decide.

---

## 2. Trust First

Trust is the marketplace currency.

- Verification before full participation  
- Before/after evidence on jobs  
- Warranties on eligible work  
- Transparent disputes  
- No dark patterns on price or urgency  

If a feature increases conversion but destroys trust, it is rejected.

---

## 3. Speed

Time-to-help matters in homes (AC in summer, plumbing leaks).

- Fast request creation  
- Fast matching waves  
- Fast offer loop  
- Fast payment confirmation  
- Fast support on failures  

Speed never bypasses security or exclusivity correctness.

---

## 4. Transparency

Customers and providers must understand what happens and why.

- Clear statuses and timelines  
- Visible fee/commission/VAT components  
- Explain sparse matching honestly  
- Show why an action is blocked  

---

## 5. Marketplace Fairness

The platform is a fair arena—not a pay-to-win lie.

- Subscription may improve priority/limits—not fake scores  
- Cheaper offers remain visible  
- Deterministic rules over arbitrary promotion  
- Anti-spam and anti-fraud for both sides  

---

## 6. Customer Protection

- Payment safety and refund paths  
- Warranty and dispute rights  
- Privacy of home and documents  
- Protection from no-shows and abuse  

---

## 7. Technician Protection

- Clear net earnings expectations  
- Protection from abusive customers where evidenced  
- Fair ranking with cold-start paths  
- Continuity to finish jobs if plan lapses mid-job  

---

## 8. Quality

- Ratings, score, claims, and completion quality drive discoverability  
- Media requirements on start/complete  
- Catalog clarity in Arabic and English  

---

## 9. Scalability

Product choices must work for millions across GCC.

- Avoid concierge-only ops bottlenecks without tooling  
- Self-serve journeys first  
- City/market configuration over hard-coded Riyadh-only assumptions long term  
- MVP may start SA-only but must not block expansion  

---

## 10. Principle Conflicts (Default Order)

1. Trust First  
2. Customer/Technician Protection (safety)  
3. Transparency  
4. Marketplace Fairness  
5. Speed  
6. Quality  
7. Scalability (long-term structure)
